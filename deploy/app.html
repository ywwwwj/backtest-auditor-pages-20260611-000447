Fake-Strong Strategy Library

Eight strategy archetypes that look good until you audit them

Use these cases to explain the pain before asking users to upload their own CSV. They are audit archetypes, not trading recommendations.

Outlier winner trap

Equity curve looks strong, but top 5 winners explain most positive return.

Cost fantasy trap

Strategy survives at 1x cost, then fails when slippage and fees are stressed.

Second-half decay

Performance is concentrated in the tuned period and weakens after the split.

Small sample confidence

High Sharpe appears on too few trades or return samples to trust.

Drawdown mismatch

Total return exists, but drawdown is too large relative to reward.

No timestamp audit gap

Without dates, regime and decay analysis cannot be trusted.

Factor clone illusion

The strategy may just be momentum, value, carry, defensive, or market beta under a new label.

Regime one-hit wonder

A rule works in one volatility or liquidity regime, then collapses when the market state changes.

Parameter mining trap

Many nearby parameter choices fail, so the winning setting may be accidental.
