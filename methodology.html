Methodology and trust

We do not predict returns. We audit whether a backtest deserves more research time.

Backtest Auditor is a credibility layer for research results exported from other tools. It looks for fragile patterns that often make a profitable-looking curve less trustworthy.

What we check and why it matters

01

Top-5 winner concentration

If a small number of positive samples explain most of the profit, the strategy may be luck-driven or too dependent on rare events.

Retest: remove the top winners and rerun the result.
02

Cost and slippage stress

Many strategies look good only under optimistic execution assumptions. Fees, spread, market impact, and slippage can erase the edge.

Retest: run 2x and 3x cost assumptions.
03

OOS and second-half decay

A curve that performs well early and then fades may be overfit to the tuned period instead of expressing a robust signal.

Retest: freeze parameters and run walk-forward splits.
04

Sample confidence

High Sharpe or high return on too few samples can be a statistical mirage. Thin samples do not justify heavy optimization.

Retest: extend history or test another market regime.
05

Drawdown mismatch

Return alone is not enough. If drawdown is large relative to reward, the strategy may be psychologically or operationally unusable.

Retest: audit lower leverage and risk caps.
06

Timestamp audit gap

Without date or time data, regime analysis, decay checks, and calendar-based retests become weaker.

Retest: export date, equity, pnl, return, and trades.

Professional data strategy

How the included review becomes more professional over time

The product should not learn from random conversations. It should learn from repeatable audit artifacts that can be checked, compared, and reused.

Input

Minimum evidence standard

Date or period, equity or returns, pnl when available, trade count, fee/slippage assumptions, and strategy notes.

Cases

Benchmark failure corpus

Outlier winner, cost fantasy, OOS decay, thin sample, drawdown mismatch, and missing timestamp cases become reusable comparison examples.

Memory

Private client context

Rulebook, risk boundary, review cadence, decisions, retest outcomes, and kill reasons are saved as customer-specific evidence.

Loop

Learning log analysis

Every blocked retest, unclear note, skipped task, and changed verdict is converted into a bottleneck the next review can address.

Benchmark corpus

We calibrate reviews against mainstream quant evidence, not isolated screenshots.

Trust boundary

This is research credibility software, not investment advice.

  • We do not predict future returns.
  • We do not tell users what to buy, sell, or trade.
  • We do not make a strategy safe or live-ready.
  • We help researchers find fragile backtests faster and decide what evidence is missing.
